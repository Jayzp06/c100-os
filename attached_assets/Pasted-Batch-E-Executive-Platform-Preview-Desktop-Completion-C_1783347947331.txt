Batch E — Executive Platform Preview & Desktop Completion

Continue building from the completed Batch A–D architecture. Preserve all existing RBAC, committee privacy, attendance, member scoring, reporting, exports, desktop authentication, desktop updater architecture, historical data protections, APIs, database schema, and business logic.

This batch is intended to prepare the platform for executive demonstration while minimizing implementation risk. Prioritize architectural completeness, polished user experience, and reuse of existing functionality over adding large amounts of new business logic.

Do not introduce breaking changes.

Do not duplicate services.

Reuse all existing APIs, reports, exports, and calculations wherever possible.

Primary Objective

Complete the desktop experience and create a polished Executive Board platform that demonstrates the long-term vision of the application while remaining stable for presentation.

Focus on architecture, navigation, dashboards, and integration rather than implementing every future workflow.
